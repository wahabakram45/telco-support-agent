Postpaid plan from TelcoX KSA.

**Price:** 399.00 SAR every 30 days

**Available now** (since 2026-01-01).

| Allowance | Amount |
|---|---|

| SMS | 1000 |
| Roaming data | 10 GB in UAE, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar |

**Included features:**

- Free calls to TelcoX numbers
