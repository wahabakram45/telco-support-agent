Postpaid plan from TelcoX UAE.

**Price:** 425.00 AED every 30 days

**Available now** (since 2026-01-01).

| Allowance | Amount |
|---|---|
| Local data | Unlimited (full speed up to 500 GB, then reduced speed) |
| Local minutes | 5000 |
| SMS | 1000 |
| Roaming data | 10 GB in Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar |

**Included features:**
- 5G
- Free calls to TelcoX numbers
- Airport lounge access
