# Prepaid FAQs

### How do I check my prepaid balance?
You can check your balance by dialling **\*100#**.

### Where can I recharge my prepaid?
You can recharge in the **TelcoX app**, on the **website**, or at **retail stores**.

### What happens if my balance is too low when my plan renews?
If your balance is too low at renewal, a **7-day grace period** starts. During this time, **only incoming calls** work.

### Does my prepaid plan roll over unused data?
No. **Prepaid plans do not roll over unused data**, so any unused data is not carried to the next period.

### Can I get a refund after I buy a prepaid plan?
No. **Prepaid plan purchases cannot be refunded after activation**.

### How long does it take to activate an eSIM in the app?
eSIM activation in the app **usually takes about 10 minutes**.

### Why did my plan change immediately, or only later?
**Upgrades take effect immediately**. **Downgrades take effect at the next renewal**. If you’re not sure what you changed, this timing is usually the clue.

### Why did my internet or service stop working?
If your plan renewal happened and your balance was too low, the **7-day grace period** may be in effect. During that grace period, **only incoming calls work**—so you may notice reduced service compared to normal.

### I still have data left—will it stay for next time?
With prepaid, **unused data doesn’t roll over**. So it won’t carry into your next plan period.
