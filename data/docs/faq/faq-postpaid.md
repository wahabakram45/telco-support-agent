# Postpaid FAQs

### When do I get my bill?
Bills are issued on the **1st of each month in KSA** and on the **5th in the UAE**.

### What happens if I pay late?
If you don’t pay on time, the late payment fee is **25 SAR in KSA** and **30 AED in the UAE**.

### Are electronic bills free?
Yes. **Electronic bills are free**.

### Is VAT included in the price?
It depends on where you are:
- **KSA:** VAT is **included** in prices.
- **UAE:** VAT is **added on top**.

### Why did my data roll over (or not)?
If your postpaid plan is **priced at 150 or more per month**, you can **roll over up to 50% of unused data** to the next cycle. If your plan is below that price point, this roll-over rule doesn’t apply.

### My internet feels slow—could it be my data?
Data speeds can vary for many reasons, but if you’re asking about your allowance, the key point is whether your plan qualifies for roll over. For plans **150 or more per month**, you can carry over **up to 50% of unused data** to the next cycle.

### Can I cancel my postpaid line?
Yes. You can cancel your postpaid line with **30 days’ notice**.

### Do I need to pay VAT twice or something?
No—VAT treatment is just different by market:
- In **KSA**, VAT is already **included** in the prices you see.
- In the **UAE**, VAT is **added on top**.

### How much will my bill be?
Your bill date and the VAT rules depend on your market:
- **KSA:** bills go out on the **1st**, and VAT is **included**.
- **UAE:** bills go out on the **5th**, and VAT is **added on top**.

If you’re checking your bill, also remember that electronic bills are **free**, and late payments can trigger a fee (**25 SAR** in KSA / **30 AED** in the UAE).
