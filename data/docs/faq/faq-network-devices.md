# Network and Device FAQs

### Where is 5G actually available?
5G is available in **Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam** in **KSA**. In the **UAE**, it’s available in **Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah**.

### Why did my 5G not work even though I’m in a 5G area?
To use 5G, you need a **5G-capable device** and a **plan with the 5G feature**. Also, 5G has **no extra charge**, but it still requires those two conditions.

### Does 5G cost extra?
No. **5G has no extra charge**. You just need the right device and a plan that includes the **5G feature**.

### Can I use my mobile internet on my laptop (tethering)?
Yes. **Tethering is allowed on all plans**.

### Is tethering unlimited?
Not on unlimited plans. On **unlimited plans**, tethering is **capped at 20 GB per cycle**.

### Why did my unlimited internet get slower?
On **unlimited plans**, after you pass the **fair usage threshold**, your speed drops to **1 Mbps**.

### What should I do if I lost my SIM?
If your SIM is lost, you should **block it immediately** in the app, or by calling **900**. This helps prevent someone else from using it.
