# Roaming FAQs

### Why is roaming data not working on my new SIM?
On new lines, **data roaming is disabled by default**. If you want to use data while you’re outside your home network, you’ll need to turn roaming on first.

### How do I turn roaming on?
You can turn roaming on in either of these ways:
- In the **TelcoX app**
- By **dialling *123#**

### What is the GCC Day Pass, and how much data do I get?
The **GCC Day Pass** gives you **1 GB for 24 hours**. The price depends on where you are:
- **25 SAR in KSA**
- **20 AED in the UAE**

### Why did my roaming data stop suddenly?
Roaming data is **suspended at 500 SAR in KSA or 400 AED in the UAE per billing cycle**, unless you **opt out in the app**. This means once you reach that amount for the billing cycle, your roaming data can be paused.

### Can I avoid the roaming data suspension?
Yes. You can **opt out in the app**. If you opt out, the roaming data suspension at **500 SAR (KSA)** or **400 AED (UAE)** per billing cycle won’t apply.

### Is there a way to buy roaming data for specific countries?
Yes. **Roaming add-on plans** give a **fixed amount of roaming data** in **specific countries**. To see what’s available and the exact data amounts, check the **add-on plan pages**.

### Why did my roaming internet get slow or stop?
Roaming performance can be affected by many things, but if your service stopped, it may be related to the **roaming data suspension thresholds**: **500 SAR in KSA** or **400 AED in the UAE** per billing cycle (unless you opted out in the app). Also, remember that on **new lines**, data roaming starts **disabled by default** until you turn it on.
