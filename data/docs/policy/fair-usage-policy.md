# Fair Usage Policy

This Fair Usage Policy explains how “unlimited” data is managed on unlimited data plans. It helps keep networks reliable for all customers. The key point is that unlimited plans provide full-speed data up to a fair usage threshold in each billing cycle.

## Full-speed data before the threshold

Unlimited data plans give full-speed data up to the plan’s fair usage threshold in each billing cycle. The fair usage threshold for each plan is listed on its plan page.

## What happens after the threshold

Once you reach the fair usage threshold, your speed is reduced to **1 Mbps** until the plan renews. The threshold resets when the plan renews.

After the threshold is reached, **video streaming may be limited to 480p**.

## Tethering (mobile hotspot) limits

If you use tethering (a mobile hotspot) on unlimited plans, it is capped at **20 GB per billing cycle**. Tethering data also **counts toward the fair usage threshold**.

## Where to check your threshold

Because each plan can have a different fair usage threshold, you should check the exact threshold on the plan page for your specific unlimited data plan.

## Markets covered

This policy applies to **all customers in KSA and the UAE**. Where a fact differs by market, both versions apply.
