# Roaming Policy (KSA)

**Saudi Arabia (KSA) customers only.** This page explains how roaming works for customers in Saudi Arabia (KSA). Roaming lets you use your mobile service while you’re outside your home network.

## Roaming data pricing (pay-as-you-go)

If you use pay-as-you-go roaming data, the cost depends on the country you’re roaming in:

- **GCC countries:** **30 SAR per 100 MB**
- **All other countries:** **60 SAR per 100 MB**

## GCC Day Pass

If you prefer a set allowance, you can use the **GCC Day Pass**:

- **Cost:** **25 SAR**
- **Included data:** **1 GB of roaming data**
- **Validity:** **24 hours**

## Incoming calls while roaming

When you receive calls while roaming, the charge is:

- **3 SAR per minute**

## How to activate data roaming

**Data roaming is disabled by default on new lines.**
To start using data roaming, you can activate it in either of these ways:

- In the **TelcoX app**, or
- By **dialling *123#**

## Spending limit and suspension

To help control roaming usage, **data roaming is suspended when roaming charges reach 500 SAR in a billing cycle**.

- The suspension happens **unless you opt out of this limit in the app**.

If your data roaming is suspended, you may need to adjust your limit settings in the TelcoX app before you can use roaming data again.
