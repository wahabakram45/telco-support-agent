# Contacting Customer Support

Need help with your TelcoX service? You can reach Customer Support in a few different ways, depending on what’s most convenient for you.

**Chat support in the TelcoX app (24/7)**
You can chat with us anytime through the TelcoX app. This service is available **24/7**, so you can get help day or night.

**Call Customer Support from your TelcoX line**
If you’re calling from a TelcoX line, you can dial **900**. Calling **900** from a TelcoX line is **free**.

**Call from other phones**
If you’re using a phone that isn’t a TelcoX line, you can still call Customer Support. Dial **800 1234 567** from other phones.

**Visit a TelcoX store**
If you prefer face-to-face support, you can visit a TelcoX store. Stores are open **from 10:00 to 22:00**, **Saturday to Thursday**.

Choose the option that works best for you—chat for quick help in the app, calls for direct support, or a store visit if you want in-person assistance.
