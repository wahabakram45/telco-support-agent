# ID Requirements for New Lines

To register a new mobile line, you need to provide the right identification. The exact ID depends on where you’re registering: KSA or the UAE. Having the correct documents helps you complete setup without delays.

## ID needed for new lines in KSA
In KSA, you must provide a **National ID** or an **Iqama** that is **verified by fingerprint**.

## ID needed for new lines in the UAE
In the UAE, you must provide an **Emirates ID**.

## How many lines can be registered to one ID
A **maximum of 5 lines** can be registered to **one ID**. This limit applies when registering lines under the same ID.

## Visitor prepaid lines (passport and visa)
If you are a visitor, you can get a **prepaid line** using:
- a **passport**
- a **valid visa**

Visitor lines are **prepaid only**.

## How long visitor lines stay active
Visitor prepaid lines **stay active for 30 days**. They will remain active for this period unless they are **renewed**.

If you’re planning to use a line temporarily while visiting, these rules explain how long you can expect your prepaid service to remain active.
