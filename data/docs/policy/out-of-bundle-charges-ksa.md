# Charges Outside Your Bundle (KSA)

If you’re a **Saudi Arabia (KSA) customer**, this page explains what happens when you use services **outside your bundle** (after your included allowance is used up).

## Local calls (after bundle minutes)
Once your bundle’s minutes are used, **local calls cost 0.5 SAR per minute**.

## SMS (after bundle allowance)
Once your bundle’s SMS allowance is used, **each SMS costs 0.25 SAR**.

## Mobile data (when your bundle data runs out)
When your bundle’s data runs out, **mobile data stops**.

This is the default behaviour unless **Data Overage** is enabled.

## Data Overage (extra data)
If **Data Overage is enabled**, you can continue using mobile data after your bundle runs out. In this case, **extra data costs 5 SAR per 100 MB**.

## Change Data Overage in the TelcoX app
You can **switch Data Overage on or off in the TelcoX app at any time**.

If you’re trying to avoid unexpected charges, it helps to check whether Data Overage is currently enabled before you use more data.
