# Changing Your Plan

You can change your mobile plan whenever you need. How and when the change takes effect depends on whether you’re upgrading or downgrading, and on the type of service you’re moving between (prepaid or postpaid).

## Upgrading to a more expensive plan
If you upgrade to a more expensive plan, the change takes effect immediately. You’ll pay the prorated difference for the rest of the cycle, so you’re only charged for the remaining time in your current billing period.

## Downgrading to a less expensive plan
If you downgrade to a less expensive plan, the change takes effect at the next renewal.

There’s also a limit on how often you can downgrade:
- Only one downgrade is allowed per billing cycle.

## Switching between prepaid and postpaid
If you’re moving from prepaid to postpaid, a credit check is required. This means the switch depends on the outcome of that check.

## Data after the change
When you change your plan, unused data from the old plan is not carried over after the plan change. So if you have remaining data at the time of switching, it won’t roll into your new plan.

## Plan changes can affect timing and charges
Because upgrades take effect immediately (with prorated charges) and downgrades take effect at the next renewal, it’s worth thinking about when you want the new plan to start. Also, remember that unused data won’t be carried over, so your new plan’s data allowance will be the one you use going forward.
