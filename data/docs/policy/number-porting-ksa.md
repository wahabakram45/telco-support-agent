# Moving Your Number to TelcoX (KSA)

This information is for **Saudi Arabia (KSA) customers only**. If you want to keep your current mobile number and move it to TelcoX, you can do that through number portability (also called “porting”).

## Cost to move your number
Moving a number to TelcoX from another operator is **free**.

## How long it takes
Porting usually takes **up to 3 working days**. During this time, your number is being transferred to TelcoX.

## What you must have with your previous operator
To be able to port your number, you must meet these requirements:

- You must have **no unpaid bills** with the previous operator.
- The **name on your ID must match** the name registered with the previous operator.

If either of these doesn’t match, your porting request may not be possible.

## After you activate with TelcoX
If you later want to move your number **out of TelcoX**, you can only do this **after 60 days from activation**.

## Helpful tip
Porting works best when your details are consistent. Make sure the name on your ID is the same as what your previous operator has on file, and that any outstanding payments are cleared before you request the transfer.
