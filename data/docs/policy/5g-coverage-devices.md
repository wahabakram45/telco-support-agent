# 5G Coverage and Devices

5G can deliver faster speeds and a better mobile experience, but it only works where coverage is available and on the right device and plan. This page explains where 5G is available in our markets, what you need to use it, and what happens if you move outside 5G coverage.

## 5G coverage locations

5G availability depends on your location. In our markets, 5G is available in these cities:

- **KSA:** Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam
- **UAE:** Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah

## What you need to use 5G

To use 5G, you need both of the following:

- A **5G-capable device**
- A **plan that includes the 5G feature**

If either of these isn’t in place, you won’t be able to connect to 5G.

## Cost of 5G

There is **no extra charge for 5G**. If your plan includes the 5G feature and your device is 5G-capable, you can use 5G where it’s available without paying an additional 5G fee.

## If 5G isn’t available

If you’re in an area where **5G is unavailable**, your phone will **switch to 4G automatically**. This helps you stay connected even when you move out of 5G coverage.
