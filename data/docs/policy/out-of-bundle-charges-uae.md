# Charges Outside Your Bundle (UAE)

This page is for **UAE customers only**. If you use your allowance before the end of your bundle period, these charges may apply for services outside your bundle.

## Local calls after your minutes run out

If you’ve used all your bundle minutes, **local calls** cost **0.4 AED per minute**.

## SMS after your allowance is used

If you’ve used all your bundle SMS allowance, each additional **SMS** costs **0.2 AED**.

## Mobile data when your bundle data runs out

When your bundle **data** runs out, **mobile data stops**. This means you won’t be able to use mobile data until your bundle renews—**unless** Data Overage is enabled.

## Data Overage (extra data)

If **Data Overage** is enabled, you can continue using mobile data after your bundle data runs out. In that case, extra data costs **4 AED per 100 MB**.

## Switching Data Overage on or off

You can **switch Data Overage on or off in the TelcoX app at any time**. This gives you control over whether your data continues after your bundle is used up, and whether the extra data charges apply.
