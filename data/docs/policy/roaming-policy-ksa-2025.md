# Roaming Policy (KSA) — 2025

**Saudi Arabia (KSA) customers only.** This page explains how roaming works for KSA customers in 2025. Roaming lets you use your mobile service while you’re outside your home network.

## Activating roaming
You must activate data roaming before you can use roaming services.

You can activate roaming:
- in the **TelcoX app**, or
- by **dialling *123#**.

## Roaming data (pay-as-you-go)
If you use pay-as-you-go data roaming, the cost depends on where you are:

- **GCC countries:** **40 SAR per 100 MB**
- **All other countries:** **75 SAR per 100 MB**

## GCC Day Pass
If you prefer a bundle for a short trip, you can use the **GCC Day Pass**.

- Price: **35 SAR**
- Included data: **500 MB** of roaming data
- Validity: **24 hours**

## Incoming calls while roaming
Incoming calls have a roaming charge of **3 SAR per minute** while you’re roaming.

## Spending limit and suspension
To help control roaming charges, **data roaming is suspended when roaming charges reach 300 SAR in a billing cycle**.

This means once your roaming charges hit that level during the billing cycle, your roaming data service will stop until the suspension condition no longer applies.
