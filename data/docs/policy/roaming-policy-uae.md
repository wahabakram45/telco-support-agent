# Roaming Policy (UAE)

**UAE customers only.** This policy explains how roaming works for customers with UAE mobile lines.

When you travel outside your home network, you may use voice calls and data while roaming. Roaming charges depend on where you’re travelling and on the type of roaming data you use.

## Roaming data (pay-as-you-go)

If you use pay-as-you-go roaming data, the cost is:

- **25 AED per 100 MB** in **GCC countries**
- **50 AED per 100 MB** in **all other countries**

## GCC Day Pass

You can also use the **GCC Day Pass** for roaming data:
- **20 AED for 1 GB**
- **Valid for 24 hours**

## Incoming calls while roaming

If you receive calls while roaming, incoming calls cost:
- **2.5 AED per minute**

## Roaming data availability (how to turn it on)

**Data roaming is disabled by default on new lines.** This means you need to enable it before you can use roaming data.

You can activate roaming in either of these ways:
- In the **TelcoX app**, or
- By **dialling *123#**

## Spending limit and suspension

To help control roaming charges, **data roaming is suspended when roaming charges reach 400 AED in a billing cycle**.

This suspension applies **unless you opt out of this limit in the app**. If you choose to opt out, the 400 AED suspension rule will not stop your roaming data in that billing cycle.
