# Prepaid Recharge, Renewal and Validity

This page explains how prepaid recharges work on TelcoX, including automatic renewal, grace periods, and what happens if there isn’t enough balance. It also covers recharge options and how to check your balance.

## How to recharge your prepaid line
You can recharge your prepaid line in any of these ways:
- In the TelcoX app
- On the website
- At retail stores

## Automatic plan renewal at the end of validity
A prepaid plan renews automatically at the end of its validity period, as long as your balance is enough to pay for the plan.

If your balance is not enough when the validity ends, your line enters a **7-day grace period**.

## Grace period and incoming calls
During the **7-day grace period**, only incoming calls work. Outgoing services are not included during this time.

## Deactivation after no recharge
If your line still has no recharge for **90 days after the grace period**, the line is **deactivated**.

## Main balance validity
If you have unused main balance, it does not expire while the line is active. This means you can keep your unused main balance as long as your line remains active.

## Check your balance
You can check your balance by dialling **\*100#**.

If you’re unsure whether you have enough balance to renew your prepaid plan, checking your balance before your plan’s validity ends can help you avoid the grace period.
