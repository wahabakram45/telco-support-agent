# Data Rollover

If you don’t use all your mobile data in a month, you may be able to carry some of it into the next billing cycle. This is called **data rollover**. It helps you make the most of your monthly allowance—especially if your usage varies from month to month.

## Which plans support rollover

Data rollover applies to **postpaid plans** priced at **150 SAR or 150 AED per month or more**.

If your plan is not in this price range, or if you are on a different type of plan, rollover may not be available.

## How much data can roll over

For eligible postpaid plans, **up to 50% of the monthly data allowance** can roll over.

Rolled-over data can only be carried over **to the next cycle only**. That means it cannot be saved for more than one additional billing cycle.

## How rollover is used

When the next billing cycle starts, your data is used in this order:
- **Rolled-over data is used first**
- Then **the new cycle’s data** is used

This order is important because it determines what counts toward your usage before your fresh monthly allowance begins.

## Plans that do not roll over

Data rollover is not available for:
- **Prepaid plans**
- **Unlimited plans**

If you’re on a prepaid or unlimited plan, any unused data does not carry into the next cycle.

## What this means for you

Rollover can be useful if you sometimes don’t use your full monthly allowance. Just remember that the rollover limit is **up to 50%**, it applies **only to the next cycle**, and it works only for **postpaid plans priced at 150 SAR or 150 AED per month or more**.
