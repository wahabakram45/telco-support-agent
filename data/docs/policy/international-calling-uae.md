# International Calling Rates (UAE)

**UAE customers only.** This page explains the international calling rates for calls made from your TelcoX mobile service to other countries.

International calling rates are charged **per minute**. The rate you pay depends on the destination country.

### Calls to GCC countries
Calls to **GCC countries** cost **1.2 AED per minute**.

### Calls to India, Pakistan and Bangladesh
Calls to **India, Pakistan and Bangladesh** cost **0.8 AED per minute**.

### Calls to the UK and the US
Calls to the **UK** and the **US** cost **1.0 AED per minute**.

### Calls to all other countries
Calls to **all other countries** cost **2.5 AED per minute**.

### International Saver add-on
If you make international calls to certain destinations, you may prefer the **International Saver** add-on.

- Cost: **25 AED** for **100 international minutes**
- Validity: **30 days**
- Includes calls to: **India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, the Philippines and Egypt**

### How to choose the right option
If you mostly call one of the listed countries, the per-minute international rates above may be enough. If you want a bundle of minutes for the supported destinations, the International Saver add-on provides **100 international minutes** for **25 AED**, valid for **30 days**.

If you’re unsure which option suits your calling habits, check which destination countries you call most often and compare the per-minute rate to the add-on minutes and validity.
