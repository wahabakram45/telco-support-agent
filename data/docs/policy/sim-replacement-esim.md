# SIM Replacement and eSIM

If your SIM stops working, gets lost, or you want to switch devices, you can replace your SIM or use eSIM. This page explains how replacements work for all customers in KSA and the UAE. If a rule is different by market, both versions are shown.

## Physical SIM replacement

A physical SIM replacement is free once every 12 months.

After that, further replacements cost:
- **KSA:** 25 SAR
- **UAE:** 25 AED

For any physical SIM replacement, a **valid ID is required**.

## eSIM activation

eSIM lets you activate mobile service without a physical SIM card. You can activate eSIM in the **TelcoX app**.

Activation **usually takes about 10 minutes**.

For any eSIM activation, a **valid ID is required**.

## Moving eSIM to a new device

You can move an eSIM to a new device **at most 3 times per month**. If you reach this limit, you may need to wait until the next month to move it again.

## If your SIM is lost or stolen

If your SIM is lost or stolen, block it **immediately** to help protect your service:
- Block it in the **TelcoX app**, or
- Call **900**

Blocking quickly can help reduce the risk of unauthorized use while you arrange a replacement.
