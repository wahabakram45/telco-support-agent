# Postpaid Billing and Payments (UAE)

This page is for **UAE customers only**. If you have a TelcoX postpaid plan, here’s how your monthly billing and payments work.

## When your postpaid bill is issued
Your postpaid bill is issued on the **5th of each month**. Your bill amount is based on your plan and usage as shown on the invoice.

## Payment due date
Payment is due within **20 days of the bill date**.

If you don’t pay by the due date, a **late payment fee of 30 AED** applies after the due date.

## What happens if you still haven’t paid
If your bill remains unpaid **40 days after the bill date**, your **outgoing services are suspended**.

This is to help protect your account from ongoing unpaid charges.

## Electronic vs paper bills
You can choose how you receive your bill:

- **Electronic bills are free**
- **Paper bills cost 10 AED per month**

## Taxes (VAT)
In the UAE, **VAT of 5%** is added on top of the advertised plan prices.

## How to pay your bill
You can pay your postpaid bill using any of the following options:

- In the **TelcoX app**
- By **card**
- At **TelcoX stores**

After you pay, keep an eye on your account status to confirm your services are active (especially if your payment was made close to the suspension timeline).
