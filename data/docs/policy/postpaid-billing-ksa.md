# Postpaid Billing and Payments (KSA)

This page is for **Saudi Arabia (KSA) customers only**. If you’re on a postpaid plan, this explains when your bill is issued, when it’s due, and what happens if payment is late.

## When your bill is issued
Your **postpaid bill is issued on the 1st of each month**.

## Payment due date
Your payment is due **within 21 days of the bill date**. If you pay after the due date, a late payment fee may apply.

## Late payment fee
A **late payment fee of 25 SAR** applies **after the due date**.

## Service suspension for unpaid bills
If your bill is unpaid **45 days after the bill date**, your **outgoing services are suspended**. This means you won’t be able to make outgoing calls or use outgoing services until the bill is paid (based on the rules above).

## Electronic vs paper bills
- **Electronic bills are free**
- **Paper bills cost 10 SAR per month**

Choosing electronic billing can help you avoid the monthly paper bill cost.

## VAT in plan prices
A **VAT of 15% is already included in all advertised plan prices in KSA**. This means the plan prices you see already include VAT.

## How to pay your bill
You can pay your postpaid bill using any of these options:
- The **TelcoX app**
- **Bank transfer**
- **SADAD**

If you want to avoid late charges or suspension, it’s best to make sure your payment is completed **within 21 days of the bill date**.
