# International Calling Rates (KSA)

**Saudi Arabia (KSA) customers only.** This page shows our international calling rates for outgoing calls from KSA.

International rates are charged **per minute**. The cost depends on the destination country (or group of countries) you’re calling.

### Calls to GCC countries
If you’re calling a **GCC country**, the rate is **1.5 SAR per minute**.

### Calls to India, Pakistan and Bangladesh
If you’re calling **India, Pakistan or Bangladesh**, the rate is **0.9 SAR per minute**.

### Calls to the UK and the US
If you’re calling the **UK or the US**, the rate is **1.2 SAR per minute**.

### Calls to all other countries
If you’re calling **any other country**, the rate is **3 SAR per minute**.

### International Saver add-on
If you want a more predictable option for certain destinations, you can add **International Saver**.

- Cost: **30 SAR**
- Included minutes: **100 international minutes**
- Validity: **30 days**
- Destinations covered: **India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, the Philippines and Egypt**

This add-on is designed for calls to the listed countries during the validity period. If you dial a country that isn’t included in the add-on, standard international rates apply based on the destination category above.
