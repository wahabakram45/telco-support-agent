# Refunds and Cancellation

If you’re thinking about cancelling your service or requesting a refund, it helps to know what happens next. This page explains the key rules for refunds and cancellations for customers in **KSA and the UAE**.

## Refunds for prepaid plans
If you buy a **prepaid plan**, the purchase **cannot be refunded after the plan is activated**.
That means the time to check your plan choice is before activation.

## Cancelling postpaid lines
If you have a **postpaid line**, you can cancel it with **30 days’ notice**.

If you’re on a **12-month postpaid contract**, cancelling it early has an extra cost: you will be charged **50% of the remaining monthly fees**.

## Device instalments
If you have **device instalments** linked to your postpaid line, you must pay them **in full when the postpaid line is cancelled**.
This is important to plan for, because it applies even if you cancel your line with notice.

## Refund processing time
If your refund request is approved, **refunds are processed within 14 working days**.

## What to expect
Cancellation and refunds can involve more than one part of your account—your service line, any contract term, and any device instalments. If you want to avoid surprises, make sure you check:
- whether your plan has already been activated (prepaid),
- whether you’re within a 12-month contract (postpaid),
- and whether device instalments are still outstanding.
